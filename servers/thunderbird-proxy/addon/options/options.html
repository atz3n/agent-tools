Thunderbird Proxy Bridge

The bridge polls the thunderbird-proxy for commands; the proxy verifies the add-on with its own THUNDERBIRD_ADDON_TOKEN — never the agent-facing THUNDERBIRD_PROXY_TOKEN.

The add-on token is the THUNDERBIRD_ADDON_TOKEN of the proxy's secrets/ file; the response secret is its THUNDERBIRD_ADDON_SECRET. The proxy signs every add-on-face response with the secret and this add-on refuses to execute anything it cannot verify — a look-alike service on the same port gets nowhere.

Hard deadline for a single Thunderbird operation — a wedged call (e.g. a rename stuck on a server prompt) is reported as failed after this long instead of blocking the bridge. Gmail IMAP moves take ~20-30 s, so stay above your slowest operation; keep it below the proxy's COMMAND_TIMEOUT (default 75 s). Blank uses 60 s.
